Saved places

Sea-level pressure is converted to true station pressure using that place's altitude before it reaches the solver. Search fills altitude from terrain data — check it, terrain models round off. Wind is a 10 m model wind for the grid square, not what's at your muzzle. Treat it as a starting number, never as the call.

Reticle holds — TMOA

18× only. The TMOA is a second-focal-plane reticle, so the hash marks only subtend 1 MOA at maximum magnification. Every yardage below is wrong at any other power — at 10× a hash is worth 1.8 MOA, not 1.

Holds are measured from your 100 yd zero with the turret at the bottom — dial and hold at the same time and these numbers no longer apply. The wind column is MOA at the speed set on the main screen, so it reads straight across onto the horizontal hashes. Rows below the expansion floor are dimmed.
